# Extreme City Life (Traffic & Pedestrians)

Turns Vice City into a bustling, active 1980s metropolis.

### Features:
- Up to 300% car density on Washington Beach, Starfish Island, Ocean Drive, and Downtown.
- Up to 300% pedestrian density with diverse ped models and roller skaters.
- Anti-despawn vehicle pool safeguard: nearby cars don't vanish into thin air when turning the camera around.
- Active police patrols and realistic traffic flow.
