# Vice City HD Remake Essentials

This mod is active by default in **Leonidas ViceCityStories (LVCS)**.

### Features:
- **Atmospheric Timecycle Remaster**: Vibrant 80s pastel colors, rich sunset oranges, deep purples, and radiant turquoise ocean.
- **Extended Draw Distance**: LOD distances increased by up to 300% without pop-in.
- **High Resolution Particles & Sky**: Crisper rain streaks, smooth clouds, and clearer stars.
- **Remastered Presets**: Full compatibility with 4K and Ultrawide internal resolutions.
