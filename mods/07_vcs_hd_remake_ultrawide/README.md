# GTA Vice City Stories HD Remake (16:9 & Ultrawide)

**Next-Generation Remake Overhaul for Leonidas Vice City Stories (LVCS)**

Developed specifically for PC displays ranging from standard 16:9 full HD / 4K monitors to 21:9 Ultrawide and 32:9 Super Ultrawide panoramic setups.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Dynamic Hor+ Widescreen & Ultrawide Engine
- **Field of View (FOV)**: Native Hor+ calculation scales view frustum dynamically without vertical cropping (Vert-) or anamorphic distortion.
- **Aspect Ratio Compatibility**:
  - `16:9` (1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3840x2160 4K)
  - `16:10` (1920x1200, 2560x1600 Steam Deck / Pro monitors)
  - `21:9` Ultrawide (2560x1080, 3440x1440, 5120x2160)
  - `32:9` Super Ultrawide (3840x1080, 5120x1440 Dual QHD)
- **Cutscenes**: Automatic letterbox removal displays panoramic cinematics in full screen.

### 2. Screen-Edge Anchored HUD
- Radar mini-map placed snugly at the bottom-left screen edge.
- Health, armor, wanted stars, and active weapon icons positioned cleanly at the top-right.
- Subtitles, mission titles, and tutorial tips centered with safe margins.
- Sniper, rocket launcher, and camera viewfinders dynamically centered and calibrated.

### 3. Remastered Modern Vehicle Handling (`HANDLING.CFG`)
- Rebalanced suspension dampening, higher top speeds, and responsive steering geometry.
- Authentic drift physics, counter-steering responsiveness, and high-speed stability on PC gamepads, keyboards, and racing wheels.

### 4. Vibrant 80s Miami Synthwave Palette (`CARCOLS.DAT`)
- Overhauled vehicle colors: Neon magenta, pastel turquoise, sunset coral, midnight metallic blue, pearl white, and gold.

### 5. High-Definition Timecycle & Lighting (`TIMECYC.DAT`)
- Crystal-clear 24-hour day/night cycle.
- Deep neon sunsets with violet and golden hour skies.
- Extended fog distance (3500m+) eliminating PS2 pop-in and fog walls.
- Dynamic sun coronas and volumetric night street light reflections.

### 6. Ultra Particle Effects (`PARTICLE.CFG`) & Ocean Simulation (`WATER.DAT`)
- Dense tire burnout smoke, high-velocity exhaust backfires, vibrant sparks, and explosive shockwaves.
- Crystal clear turquoise waters in the Vice City bay with realistic wave crests and reflections.
