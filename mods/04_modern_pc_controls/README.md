# Modern PC Controls & Quality of Life

Brings true modern PC gaming ergonomics to GTA Vice City Stories.

### Features:
- **PC Mouse Look**: Smooth free-look camera on foot and behind vehicles without sluggish deadzones.
- **Precision Aim**: Right-click free aim and lock-on modes.
- **Fast Weapon Switch**: Mouse wheel or 1-9 direct weapon hotkeys.
- **Automatic Intro Skip**: Direct boot into gameplay without waiting through logo animations.
- **Sprint Quality of Life**: Hold Shift or bind to toggle sprint.
