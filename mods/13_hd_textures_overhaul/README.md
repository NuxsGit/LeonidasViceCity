# HD Textures & Surfaces Overhaul (Anti-Pixel Remaster)

High-definition texture replacement pack designed specifically for the Leonidas Vice City Stories (LVCS) HD Remake Engine.

## Features:
- **No More Pixelation**: Replaces low-resolution 32x32 and 64x64 legacy PS2 textures with 1024x1024 and 512x512 anti-aliased assets.
- **Road & Ground Surfaces**: Seamless dark asphalt with procedural micro-aggregates (`road_asphalt.png`), clean sidewalk concrete paving tiles (`pavement_tiles.png`), and smooth Ocean Beach sand (`sand_beach.png`).
- **Remastered UI & HUD**: Crisp high-res transparent radar disc with cardinal points (`radardisc.png`), modern North direction pointer (`radar_north.png`), precision crosshairs (`crosshair.png`), and vector PlayStation action glyphs.
- **Environment & Lighting**: Volumetric smooth radial neon glow (`neon_glow.png`), panoramic Vice City skyline car reflections (`car_generic_reflection.png`), and tropical palm fronds with anti-aliased alpha transparency (`palm_leaves.png`).
- **Fully Integrated**: Automatically scanned and loaded into VFS by `HdTextureManager` with 16x Anisotropic Filtering and MSAA.
