# Leonidas ViceCityStories v0.4.0 - HD Remake & Universal Gamepad Release

Welcome to **Leonidas ViceCityStories (LVCS) v0.4.0**! This major release delivers the full **GTA Vice City Stories HD Remake (16:9 & Ultrawide)** mod, universal gamepad support, complete launcher redesign with the Leonidas mascot theme, in-game mod switches, and ISO asset extraction tooling.

---

## 🌟 Highlights

### 1. GTA Vice City Stories HD Remake (16:9 & Ultrawide) Mod (`mods/07_vcs_hd_remake_ultrawide/`)
- **Native Hor+ Widescreen & Ultrawide Engine**:
  - Full support for `16:9` (1080p, 1440p, 4K), `16:10` (Steam Deck, pro displays), `21:9` Ultrawide, and `32:9` Super Ultrawide.
  - Dynamically calculates the camera's view window to widen horizontal FOV without vertical cropping or distortion.
- **Screen-Edge HUD Anchoring**:
  - Mini-map radar anchored cleanly at the bottom-left screen edge.
  - Health, armor, wanted stars, and active weapon icons positioned at the top-right screen edge.
  - Subtitles and mission objectives centered with comfortable safe zones.
- **Cinematic Letterbox Removal**:
  - Automatic removal of PS2 4:3 black bars (`display.cutscene_bars = 0`) for panoramic cutscenes.
- **Overhauled Vehicle Physics & Handling (`HANDLING.CFG`)**:
  - Tuned for high-fps responsiveness, tighter suspension damping, realistic counter-steering, and drift control on Infernus, Cheetah, Banshee, Comet, bikes (PCJ-600, Sanchez), and helicopters (Hunter, Maverick).
- **Vibrant Miami Synthwave Palette (`CARCOLS.DAT`)**:
  - Leonidas signature neon pink (`#ED1E79`), electric cyan, royal purple, and sunset orange vehicle coats.
- **HD Timecycle & Atmosphere (`TIMECYC.DAT`)**:
  - 24-hour HD lighting with crystal-clear midday skies, glowing neon sunsets, and fog draw distance extended to 3500m.
- **High-Definition Particles (`PARTICLE.CFG`) & Ocean (`WATER.DAT`)**:
  - Dense burnout smoke, sparks, wake spray, and crystal-clear turquoise waters across the Vice City bay.

---

### 2. Universal Gamepad Support Mod (`mods/06_universal_gamepad_support/`)
- **Zero-Configuration Plug-and-Play**:
  - Sony PlayStation 5 (DualSense), PlayStation 4 (DualShock 4), PS3.
  - Microsoft Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Xbox 360 controllers.
  - Nintendo Switch Pro Controller & Joy-Cons.
  - Generic Bluetooth, DirectInput, and HID PC gamepads.
- **Automatic SDL3 Hotplug**:
  - Load community controller mapping database on startup; connect or disconnect controllers at any time without restarting the game.

---

### 3. In-Game Mod Management (F2 Settings Menu)
- Individual toggle switches dynamically registered in the in-game menu under **Mods & Remake** $\rightarrow$ **Installed Mods**.
- Seamlessly toggle mods on or off in real-time.

---

### 4. Launcher Redesign & Leonidas Mascot Theme
- **Fixed Mods Sheet Layout**:
  - Panel enlarged from 480px to 540px; resolved all badge and text overflowing issues.
- **Animated Leonidas Mascot**:
  - Rising synthwave sun with animated Leonidas mascot (Macca the Alligator with 80s sunglasses and tropical cocktail).
- **Brand Colors & Emblems**:
  - Official neon palette integrated across the launcher, buttons, and custom titlebar emblem.
- **Multiplayer Button**:
  - "Jouer en Multijoueur" with an interactive "BIENTÔT" neon badge preparing for the upcoming LVCS-MP online module.

---

### 5. Modding & ISO Extraction Tooling
- Added `tools/extract_mod_assets.py` to inspect PS2 ISO images, extract base data configurations, and scaffold custom mods into the VFS.

## 💾 Installation & Updating
- **Windows**: Download `LVCS-0.4.0-windows.zip`, extract and run `VCSLauncher.exe`.
- **Linux**: Download `LVCS-0.4.0-x86_64.AppImage`, make executable (`chmod +x`), and launch.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon)**: Download `LVCS-0.4.0-macos-arm64.dmg`, drag **VCSLauncher** to Applications.
